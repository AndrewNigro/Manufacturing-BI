business rules - inherited reporting notes

don't use Lost opportunities in pipeline.

stale = still open and Close Date already passed.

Order Number is created on the opportunity and follows a standard order into ERP if we win it.

original quote = Order Number.
first revision = Order Number-1.
second revision = Order Number-2, etc.
Number of Revisions counts revisions after the original.

quotes good for 30 days. if we revise after pricing changed, use current pricing for the revision.

pricing reviewed twice a year based on manufacturing costs.
list prices target about 60% margin before discounts.

volume discount:
1-9 = 0%
10-24 = 5%
25-49 = 10%
50-99 = 15%
100-199 = 20%
200-499 = 25%
500+ = 30%

accounts can have their own Standard Discount % up to 30%.
don't stack account discount and quantity discount. use whichever approved discount is better.

ship day is Friday.
lead time is shipping weeks, not 5-business-day blocks.
if the clock starts Mon-Thurs, the next Friday can be Week 1.
if it starts Friday, the following Friday is Week 1.

yard/plastic:
1-49 1 wk
50-199 2 wks
200-499 3 wks
500+ 4 wks

folding:
1-49 2 wks
50-199 3 wks
200-499 4 wks
500-999 5 wks
1000+ 6 wks

office/gaming:
1-29 2 wks
30-59 3 wks
60-89 4 wks
90-119 5 wks
120-199 6 wks
200-299 7 wks
300+ 8 wks

spare parts normally 1 wk.
mixed-family order uses the longest applicable lead time.

Net 30 only if Finance approved the account.
100% Prepay and 50/50 are account choice if they aren't using Net 30.
if credit denied, account has to use 100% Prepay or 50/50.

lead-time clock:
Net 30 = Won date
100% Prepay = Initial Payment Date
50/50 = Initial Payment Date (first 50%)

Final Payment Date is needed to see if a 50/50 order was held for account payment.
payment dollar amounts aren't part of this reporting project.

account-requested delay/change before production -> update Promised Ship Date.
company/vendor delay -> keep Promised Ship Date and use Adjusted Promised Ship Date + reason.

if we miss Promised Ship Date because of us/vendor, it's late even if there's an adjusted date.
if the order was ready on time and only final payment is holding shipment, don't count it late.
if we were already late before payment became a problem, it is still late.

split shipments allowed by order line.
order stays Partially Shipped until all shippable lines are done, then Closed.
backlog = remaining unshipped product-line Value, not full order value.

FREIGHT and TAX are dedicated line codes. remove them from product units / product sales / product margin reporting.
header Freight and Tax should reconcile to those line amounts.

historical price and COGS stay on the order line. don't replace them with today's Product Reference values.

before Production, quantity/product revisions are allowed.
once Production starts, added quantity needs a new normal sales/order process.
full cancellation before Production = no fee.
after Production starts = 20% cancellation fee.
large quantity reduction after Production can be treated like partial cancellation.
cancelled orders stay in ERP as Cancelled.

returns allowed within 90 days, starting 7 days after shipment.
opened/used is still returnable with proof of purchase.
no restocking fee.

warranty is 1 year, starting 7 days after shipment.
warranty investigation should get pictures when possible.
approved replacement orders go into ERP at $0.
warranty replacement uses a normal new sequential Order Number and Type = Warranty. no -W1 suffix.

big-box locations can have similar names. store number matters.
Purchase Order is not guaranteed unique.

if you can't tell what the correct Account/Order/Line is, flag it instead of guessing.

direct accounts need a Purchase Order once the sale is Won / entered in ERP.
if a Direct Business or Direct Individual account doesn't provide its own PO, use the Order Number as the Purchase Order.
open/lost opportunities can still have a blank Purchase Order until the business is actually won.

Push only goes up when Sales moves an open opportunity projected Close Date to a later date. moving the date earlier does not add to Push. closing the opportunity and replacing Close Date with the actual Won/Lost date is not treated as another Push.
