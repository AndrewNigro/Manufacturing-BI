reporting requirements - management asks / working list

sales pipeline
what business do we think is coming up?
need open pipeline by time period, rep, stage, Account Type, etc.
Push should be available so we can see which opportunities have been moved out repeatedly.

stale opportunities
what open opportunities are past Close Date and need follow-up?
use Push as extra context when deciding which repeatedly delayed opportunities need attention.

sales won by month / quarter / year
how much business are we winning over time?

sales rep performance
compare reps head to head. booked/won Value, shipped Value, margin %, number of wins, pipeline where useful.

weighted account ranking
which account relationships should we be building?
score should use won Value, avg margin %, Return Rate, Warranty Rate, number of won orders, and Pipeline Value.
weights still need to be finalized when report is built.

product sales
what is selling and what isn't?
Product Family / Model / Product Code, Qty, Value, margin, trends. exclude FREIGHT and TAX from core product reporting.

backlog
what does production/fulfillment need to prepare for?
use remaining unshipped product-line Value.

shipped revenue
how much product Value have we actually shipped?
use Ship Date. keep FREIGHT/TAX separate from product reporting.

margin reporting
are we making money?
margin $ and margin % by useful dimensions.

warranty claims
what product/quality issues are we having?
look at reason, product, account, trend, resolution.

returns / RMAs
are returns pointing to product issues, shipping issues, or account-choice issues?

on-time / late orders
are we shipping when promised?
company/vendor misses count late. account-payment-only exception applies only when order was ready on time.

delay report
which active/completed orders were delayed, why, what date did we move to, and did it count as late?
useful for operations follow-up, not just KPI reporting.

account concentration / repeat business
how dependent are we on a small number of accounts?
are accounts continuing to order from us?

lost opportunities / cancellations
how much business are we not converting and why?
keep Lost Opportunity reasons separate from ERP cancellation reasons.

bookings vs backlog vs shipments
full scope of Won business from CRM win -> open ERP backlog -> shipment.

common filters where they make sense:
Sales Rep
Account
Account Type
Product Family
Product
Month / Quarter / Year
Opportunity Stage
Order Status

don't force every filter onto every report.
